# Back up a seed to the card

Verify the wallet fingerprint, derivation path, and word count on ShieldSigner before committing any backup.

1. Unlock the initialized card with its PIN.
2. Select the encrypted backup flow in the official tool.
3. Confirm the destination card and backup identifier.
4. Lock the card and perform a non-destructive listing check.

Do not export a plaintext seed to a phone, cloud drive, screenshot, or clipboard. A backup is not complete until a restore test succeeds.
